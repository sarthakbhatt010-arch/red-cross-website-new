What guides us

Service is something we practise together.

Youth Red Cross at Doon University gives students a place to learn about helping others and to take part in meaningful service. Our work is rooted in care, cooperation and respect for the communities we serve.

From building first-aid awareness to sharing health information and supporting outreach, each area offers a practical way to turn concern into thoughtful action.
