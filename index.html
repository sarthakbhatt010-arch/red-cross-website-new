Who we are

Learn to help.
Grow by serving.

Youth Red Cross brings students together to build a culture of service at Doon University.

“The most useful thing we can offer a community is our attention, our time and the willingness to act.”

Through student-led activities, members can explore first aid and safety, take part in health and wellbeing awareness, and support community initiatives. The goal is simple: make service a meaningful part of campus life.

YRC is a place to learn with others, contribute at your own pace and build habits of empathy, responsibility and cooperation.
